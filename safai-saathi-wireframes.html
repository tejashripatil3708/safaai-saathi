⚡ Core Workflow State Machine

Every grievance logged by a citizen traverses an immutable state machine: RECEIVED (0h) → DISPATCHED TO WARD INSPECTOR → WORKER CLEANED (Photo Upload) → CITIZEN CROSS-VERIFY (24h Window) → RESOLVED / REOPENED. If an issue remains untouched past 24h or 72h, it triggers automated administrative escalation to the Godhra Chief Officer and District Development Officer (DDO).

📌 Screen Specifications

  • Unobstructed Ward Bar: Placed at top-4 with official app icon and horizontal scroll for Wards 1 to 7.
  • Realistic Ward-Cluster ETA: Vehicles are allotted 2–3 ward sectors with tight 2.5–3.8 km loops, providing accurate 2–8 minute ETAs.
  • Dynamic Collection Dismissal: Once citizen confirms collection, the arrival ETA card completely disappears and is replaced by "Collection Done Today".
  • Global Language Switcher: Placed in the fixed bottom navigation pill; accessible across all tabs without hiding text.

🔔 Van 500m Arrival Siren

When a waste collection vehicle enters the 500-meter radius threshold of the citizen's sector, the app fires an audio chime and triggers an in-app alert urging residents to bring segregated wet/dry bins to the doorstep.

⚡ User Actions Available

  • Tap any Ward pill to instantly pan the camera and view that ward's active vehicle telemetry.
  • Tap "Van Did Not Arrive" to log an automated missed collection ticket.
  • Tap Spark icon to open the Post-Collection verification sheet.

📊 ETA Disappearance Logic

When a citizen selects "Yes, Waste Collected":

  • The system records an entry in CitizenCollectionFeedback with timestamp and ward ID.
  • The floating bottom sheet immediately hides the ETA arrival card.
  • Replaced with a green status card: ✅ આજનો કચરો કલેક્ટ થઈ ગયો છે! (Today's Collection Complete).
  • Includes an "Undo / Change" action in case the user wants to revert status.

If 5 or more households in a 300m radius report "Missed by Van" within a 2-hour window, the system automatically triggers a contractor penalty alert on the Nagarpalika sanitation dashboard.

🔄 The 2-Way Verification Loop

Traditional grievance systems allow municipal workers to unilaterally close tickets. Safai Saathi enforces a strict two-way cross-verification:

  • Worker Stage: Sanitation worker marks spot cleaned and uploads after-photo proof. Ticket enters pending_verification.
  • Citizen Stage: The citizen who raised the complaint is prompted to inspect the spot.
  • Proof for Both Responses: If citizen confirms "Cleaned", they upload confirmation proof. If they dispute "Still Dirty", they upload evidence of remaining filth.

⏳ 24-Hour Failsafe & Reopen Rights

If a citizen does not inspect within 24 hours, the system transitions the ticket to auto_resolved to avoid blocking administrative KPIs. However, the citizen retains the right to click "Reopen Grievance" with fresh photo evidence at any time.
